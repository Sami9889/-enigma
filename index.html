Evangelion

The End Of

INFO

This is an experimental poster inspired by Neon Genesis Evangelion, exploring the series' themes of identity, isolation, existentialism and the fragile boundary between reality and perception. Religious imagery, distorted typography, surveillance motifs and fractured compositions reflect Evangelion's transformation from a seemingly conventional mecha story into a deeply psychological examination of what it means to be human.
